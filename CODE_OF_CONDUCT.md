# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this repository to be a respectful, constructive, and technically rigorous space for security engineering discussion. Participants are expected to communicate in good faith, welcome differing views, and focus feedback on code, architecture, documentation, and evidence.

## Expected behavior

Use respectful language, give actionable technical feedback, protect confidential or sensitive information, and assume good intent while asking clarifying questions. Credit contributions appropriately and avoid publishing content that could place users, systems, or data at risk.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberately disruptive conduct, and public disclosure of private credentials or unpatched security vulnerabilities are not acceptable. Do not post certificates, keys, tokens, Vault credentials, customer data, or real encrypted payloads.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may remove content or restrict participation when conduct violates these standards or creates a security risk. Security concerns should be reported privately under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in public discussions.
